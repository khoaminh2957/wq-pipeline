## Scale
Two-stage. STAGE 1 (gates, pass/fail, evaluated FIRST): the 8 hard gates are mechanically checked; ANY trip = auto-reject regardless of composite, mirroring the pipeline's non-overridable D1/D2 veto. STAGE 2 (graded, only for gate-survivors): each of the 7 dimensions is scored on a 0-4 anchored scale — 0 = absent / fails the dimension's floor; 1 = asserted but not shown; 2 = partial (one required element missing); 3 = solid (all elements present); 4 = exemplary (mechanism-consistent and evidence-cited with {path,line,quote}). Dimension contribution = (raw/4) x weight; COMPOSITE = sum over the 7 dimensions, range 0-100. Weights: 20/10/22/12/16/12/8 (sum 100), with sample-regime survival (22) the single heaviest per the task's central warning and typed-mechanism+counterparty (20) next as the pipeline's two vetoes.

## PASS threshold
SIM-ELIGIBLE requires ALL THREE: (a) zero hard-gate trips; (b) composite >= 70/100; (c) no gate-backing dimension (Dim 1 mechanism/counterparty, Dim 2 grounding, Dim 3 regime, Dim 5 construction, Dim 7 data/novelty) scores below 2/4. This refines S4.2's SIM-ELIGIBLE rule (>=4/5 debate APPROVE AND zero REJECT from D1 or D2, L136): the 8 hard gates ARE the D1/D2/D3/D4 + S4.1-L118 vetoes, and the >=70 composite is the >=4/5 quality bar. Outcomes: pass all three => SIM-ELIGIBLE (feeds config_sweep); composite <70 with no gate trip => REFINE (bank the specific low dimensions); any gate trip => REJECT and bank status=debate_rejected with the exact reason.

## 8 HARD GATES (Stage 1 — any trip = AUTO-REJECT, checked first)
1. 2. NO PERSISTENT COUNTERPARTY (Dim 1; S4.1 L118 'no counterparty = REJECT', D3): no named concrete agent class, a generic 'irrational traders'/'the market is wrong', a transient/one-off-dislocation loser, or a counterparty category that contradicts the mechanism tag (e.g. a mispricing tag with no limits-to-arbitrage bearer).
3. FABRICATED / UNGROUNDED CITATION (Dim 2; S4.6 H1 hallucination veto): a citation resolving to NEITHER the model77 pack's attached research-paper table NOR a canonical anomaly the reviewer already knows, OR a keyword-only construct match where the signal does not reproduce the paper's actual construct/sign/horizon.
4. IGNORES 2014-2023 REGIME VALIDITY (Dim 3): no expected per-sub-regime sign stated across the value winter / 2016 & 2020 momentum crashes / COVID / 2022 rate shock, OR a bare crowded classic whose ~0.4x post-publication-decay-haircut Sharpe cannot clear the USA d1 1.58 bar, OR a >=750-alphaCount field (market_volatility_index/enterprise_value/book_leverage) used as the PRIMARY bet, OR a composite whose every leg has alphaCount >=200.
5. SIGN-FISHING / UNFALSIFIABLE (Dim 4; S5 sign discipline): a stated plan to auto-adopt a theory-contradicting sign to harvest in-sample alpha, OR a 'works everywhere, always' claim with no ex-ante attenuation/reversal condition.
6. BROKEN CONSTRUCTION (Dim 5; Law 1 + S5 L166 hard-rejects): the mechanism's best expression is a bare monotone re-rank of one pre-built factor score (Law-1 null), a field-nature violation (ts_delta on the already-delta change_in_eps_surprise, or re-rank/sign-flip of the NONNEG [0,1] R2 field), a raw-signed nonlinear product, or a weighted/vec_avg-sum Stage-2/3 combiner.
7. DATA NON-EXISTENCE / LOOK-AHEAD (Dim 7; D1, the non-overridable veto): a mapped field that does not grep in the USA d1 catalog at the exact delay, a fabricated coverage number, or an earnings-event field claimed without point-in-time availability at delay=1.
8. NO_GO / HISTORY COLLISION (Dim 7; D4): the normalized formula+settings greps in state/*targets*.json history or carries a NO_GO / TESTED-NULL / TESTED-REFUTED / debate_rejected ledger status.

## 7 SCORED DIMENSIONS (Stage 2 — 0-4 each × weight, composite /100)
### [20] Typed causal mechanism + matched persistent counterparty
- Reviewer can complete, in one sentence WITHOUT restating the signal, [observable phenomenon] -> [named economic force] -> [directional forward-return claim]; a tautology ('cheap stocks outperform', 'high-surprise names keep surprising') scores 0 on the middle link (ALPHA_PIPELINE S4.1 L118).
- The force is TAGGED exactly one of {risk-premium, behavioral-mispricing, institutional/funding-friction}; the tag sets the Sharpe ceiling every downstream dimension inherits (Cochrane 2011): risk-premium => accept a modest persistent Sharpe (no arbitrage-away); mispricing/friction => MUST be paired with a limits-to-arbitrage persistence reason.
- Names a CONCRETE losing agent class (retail overreacting to salient earnings, analysts anchoring/under-reacting, leverage-constrained investors, index funds forced at reconstitution) AND a structural persistence reason from {behavioral bias, limits-to-arbitrage, institutional mandate, funding/leverage constraint, OR structural risk-bearer/hedging demand for a risk-premium tag}.
- The counterparty category MATCHES the mechanism tag (risk -> a risk-bearer who pays the premium and cannot stop; mispricing/friction -> behavioral/limits-to-arb/mandate) and is classified PERSISTENT, not transient {liquidity-shock, one-off dislocation}, with a one-line reason it survived 10 years of post-2016 crowding (Shleifer-Vishny 1997; Frazzini-Pedersen 2014). Absorbs the former triple-counted counterparty criteria into one gate.
- **score 0 =** Missing or signal-paraphrasing mechanism middle link, OR no named counterparty, OR a generic 'irrational traders' / transient-dislocation loser, OR a counterparty category that contradicts the mechanism tag — the pipeline's two absolute vetoes (auto-reject).
- **score full =** All three mechanism links present and typed, plus a specific persistent counterparty whose category matches the tag and who is structurally prevented from correcting the edge across 2014-2023.

- **score full =** Named anomaly + corpus-verifiable (or canonical) cite + a one-line justification that the signal reproduces the paper's actual construct, sign and horizon.

### [22] Sample-regime survival on USA 2014-2023 (decay + crowding)
- States the expected SIGN of the mechanism's contribution across the named 2014-2023 sub-regimes — 2014-2020 value winter, 2016 momentum crash, 2020 COVID/momentum crash, 2022 rate shock, diffuse mid-cycle — non-negative in >=4 of 5, with no single-regime PnL dependence (QUALITATIVE sign map; explicitly NOT the post-sim regime_audit 'best-2y PnL share <50%' / 'ex-best-2y sharpe >=1.0' numbers, which are S8.4 recordset outputs nobody can produce pre-sim).
- Is NOT a bare long-value or bare-trend bet that a documented sub-period directly reverses (value winter kills long-cheap; 2016/2020 crashes kill long-momentum) unless hedged by an orthogonal leg.
- Decay screen: a bare, widely-published crowded classic (plain value, plain 12-1 momentum, plain low-vol) takes a ~0.4x DIRECTIONAL down-weight on its raw expected Sharpe before comparison to the 1.58 bar — a rule-of-thumb from McLean-Pontiff ~58% post-publication RETURN decay (a high-variance cross-sectional average across ~97 anomalies, explicitly NOT an exact Sharpe multiplier); a novel interaction/conditioning carries no haircut but must state why the SPECIFIC interaction is not already crowded.
- Crowding via alphaCount (pack values): <=~115 is the fresh-LEG preference (momentum ratios 14/23, EPS-trend-R2 61, fcf_yield_times_forward_roe 111); the directional root is permitted up to ~200 WITH a mechanism justification (model77 roots on change_in_eps_surprise at 189) — deliberately NOT a hard primary<=110 gate that would reject the pack's own root; HARD-forbid a >=750 field (market_volatility_index 755 / enterprise_value 900 / book_leverage 2366) as PRIMARY, or any composite whose EVERY leg has alphaCount >=200.
- **score 0 =** No per-sub-regime sign expectation stated (mechanism ignores the sample), OR a bare crowded classic whose ~0.4x-haircut Sharpe cannot clear 1.58, OR a >=750-crowd field as the primary bet / every leg >=200 — 'a plausible root alpha whose sim is meaningless'.
- **score full =** A per-episode sign map non-negative in >=4/5 sub-regimes, a fresh-or-justified primary field, and (if a classic) an explicit credible reason the post-publication decay/crowding does not bind on this exact 10-year window.

### [12] Falsifiability + ex-ante expected-sign discipline
- Pre-registers (a) the sub-population where the effect should be STRONGEST (high-surprise, illiquid, hard-to-arbitrage names) and (b) >=1 explicit regime/cross-section where it should ATTENUATE or FLIP (low-dispersion regimes, mega-caps, post-crowding years); 'works everywhere, always' is unfalsifiable (Harvey 2017).
- The pre-registered failure conditions map to an inspectable S4.3 post-sim diagnostic (yearly consistency / cap-sector concentration), so a null sim is diagnostic rather than a HARKing opportunity.
- expected_sign (+/-) is declared ex-ante with its theory link and is consistent with the cited paper's documented direction (PEAD: positive surprise -> positive drift -> long high-delta-surprise).
- Any PLAN to auto-adopt a theory-contradicting sign is disqualifying: per S5 sign discipline, flips pay ONLY inside composites on genuinely unknown-sign dense-MATRIX legs, and a 7/7 opposite-expected-sign sweep is FLAGGED to the leader, never silently harvested.
- **score 0 =** Unfalsifiable 'works everywhere' claim with no attenuation/reversal condition, OR no ex-ante expected_sign, OR a stated plan to flip the sign to whatever works in-sample (sign-fishing).
- **score full =** A strongest-here sub-population plus at least one attenuation/reversal condition, and a theory-locked expected_sign consistent with the cited construct.

- The mechanism's BEST expression is NOT a bare monotone re-rank of one already-built factor score: model77 fields are pre-built, cross-sectionally-meaningful SCORES, so rank/zscore of a single field adds ZERO information (Law 1 master-law monotone absorption) and re-sells a factor the platform already ships; the edge MUST be a >=2-leg interaction/conditioning (multiply/if_else/divide) or an orthogonalization.

The Law-6 additive rank-blend branch is OUT-OF-PATH for this multiply-only funnel; genuinely independent orthogonal-forecast legs signal the composite is mis-specified, not an additive license.
- Legs are cross-family (surprise x value x momentum OK; momentum x momentum wastes a leg and concentrates regime risk).
- **score 0 =** Best expression is a single-field monotone re-rank (guaranteed Law-1 null), OR a field-nature violation (ts_delta on the already-delta surprise field / re-rank of the NONNEG R2 field), OR a raw-signed nonlinear product, OR a weighted/vec_avg-sum combiner.
- **score full =** A >=2-leg cross-family interaction/conditioning/orthogonalization, every product operand rank-bounded, field-natures respected, combiner matched to a confirmation relationship — the BEST operator expression of the mechanism, not the shortest.
